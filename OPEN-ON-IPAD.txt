# Open on iPad

## Your app link (turn on GitHub Pages first — see below)

https://victorwkliu.github.io/tjckw-service-membership-recorder/

Direct file:

https://victorwkliu.github.io/tjckw-service-membership-recorder/index.html

## Turn on the link (one-time, on your PC)

1. Open: https://github.com/victorwkliu/tjckw-service-membership-recorder/settings/pages
2. Under **Build and deployment** → **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**
3. Branch: **main** / folder: **/ (root)** → Save
4. Wait 1–2 minutes, then open the link above on your iPad in **Safari**

If GitHub says Pages needs a public repo or a paid plan:
- Repo Settings → Danger Zone → **Change visibility** → Public
  (Safe for privacy: the website is only the empty app. Member data stays in your Drive JSON.)

## On your iPad

1. Open the link in **Safari**
2. Share → **Add to Home Screen** (optional one-tap icon)
3. Each session: **Open from Drive** → work → **Save to Drive**

## Note

Opening index.html from the Google Drive app on iPad usually will NOT run the app. Use the web link above instead.
